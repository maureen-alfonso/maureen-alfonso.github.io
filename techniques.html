My Top 3 Closing Techniques

Proven approaches tailored to different deal stages and buyer dynamics.

Technique #1

The Outcome Confirmation Close

Summarize the business outcome they agreed to, then ask: "If we implement this by [date], will that hit your Q target?"

Best for: Deals at advanced stages where objections are minimal.

Win rate: 41%

Technique #2

The Risk + Timeline Close

"What's the cost of waiting another month vs. getting started now? And if we do nothing, what's the business risk?"

Best for: Stalled deals or procrastinators.

Win rate: 38%

Technique #3

The Mutual Close

"We're both aligned on value and timeline. The only question is terms. Can we get procurement and finance on a call to finalize?"

Best for: Deals stuck in legal/finance limbo.

Win rate: 36%
