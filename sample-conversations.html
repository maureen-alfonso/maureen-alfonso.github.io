SECTION 04

Sample Conversations

Senior Sales Executive

These examples reflect how I communicate as a Senior Sales Executive: commercially focused, buyer-aware, and consistent in moving strategic deals toward decision.

Conversation Examples

Budget Push-Back

Client: "You are 35% above another vendor. Can you match?"

Response: "Totally fair question. If you are open to it, I can walk through where the additional investment reduces implementation risk and speeds your return. If that value is not real, price should win."

Stalled Champion

Client: "Finance wants to delay this until next year."

Response: "Thanks for the transparency. Would it help if we join finance for 20 minutes and map the cost of waiting versus a phased rollout? That usually makes the decision clearer for everyone."

Competitor Undercut

Client: "Another provider is half your price."

Response: "Understood. The key question is whether both options produce the same business outcome and adoption speed. If you would like, we can compare both against your top three success metrics side by side."

Procurement RFP Path

Client: "We need a formal RFP for this budget level."

Response: "Absolutely. We can support the RFP process and still keep momentum by aligning technical scope now so your team avoids rework later."

Conversation Standards

Empathy First

Acknowledge the concern before presenting an argument.

Business Language

Keep responses tied to outcomes and operational impact.

Forward Motion

End each exchange with a specific, agreed next step.
