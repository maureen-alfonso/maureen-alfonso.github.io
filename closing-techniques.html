SECTION 02

Closing Techniques

Senior Sales Executive

As a Senior Sales Executive, I close by validating business value, reducing decision risk, and creating aligned urgency across executive, finance, and procurement stakeholders.

Core Closing Plays

The Outcome Confirmation Close

I restate the agreed business result and connect it to execution timing so commitment feels natural and immediate.

Best for: late-stage deals with strong internal alignment.

The Risk + Timeline Close

I make the cost of delay visible and contrast it with the value gained by acting now.

Best for: stalled opportunities and indecisive buying groups.

The Mutual Commitment Close

I summarize what both sides are aligned on and define the final operational step to finalize terms.

Best for: finance, legal, and procurement-heavy cycles.

Close Discipline

Never Rush

I move quickly only after confirming stakeholder readiness and decision criteria.

Never Guess

I validate hidden objections directly instead of relying on assumptions.

Always Define Next Step

I end every conversation with owner, date, and expected outcome.
