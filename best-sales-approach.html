SECTION 01

My Best Sales Approach

Senior Sales Executive

My Senior Sales Executive approach starts with business diagnosis, then moves to stakeholder alignment, value framing, and a clear decision path that keeps enterprise deals moving.

Four-Step Framework

  1. 01

    Lead With Discovery

    I begin by surfacing KPIs, operational blockers, and stakeholder incentives before discussing the solution.

  2. 02

    Reframe Around Business Impact

    I tie pain points to revenue, cost, speed, or risk so decision-makers evaluate outcomes, not features.

  3. 03

    Create a Shared Decision Path

    I align stakeholders on timeline, ownership, and milestones so every next step feels clear and intentional.

  4. 04

    Manage Risk Proactively

    I surface objections early and solve blockers before they become reasons to delay the deal.

Why It Works

Executive Relevance

Conversations stay anchored in strategic outcomes and board-level priorities.

Deal Momentum

Mutual action plans reduce drift and keep procurement, legal, and finance aligned.

Lower Friction

Clarity lowers uncertainty, which shortens cycle time and improves close quality.
