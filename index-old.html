My Sales Approach

Outcome-driven consultative selling built on deep discovery and strategic alignment.

01

Deep Discovery

I spend 60% of first call asking high-value questions about their KPIs, pain, and strategic goals. Understanding their real problem is non-negotiable.

02

Insight-Led Positioning

Rather than pitch features, I share an insight or benchmark that reframes their challenge. This shifts them from price-shopping to value-focused thinking.

03

Co-Create Next Steps

I propose a decision journey with clear milestones, not a generic demo. This builds buyer confidence and signals seriousness on my end.

04

Proactive Problem-Solving

I anticipate their objections and surface solutions before they ask. This removes friction and demonstrates deep industry knowledge.

My Top 3 Closing Techniques

Tested approaches that close 34% more deals than industry average.

Technique #1

The Outcome Confirmation Close

Summarize the business outcome they agreed to, then ask: "If we implement this by [date], will that hit your Q target?"

Best for: Deals at advanced stages where objections are minimal.

Win rate: 41%

Technique #2

The Risk + Timeline Close

"What's the cost of waiting another month vs. getting started now? And if we do nothing, what's the business risk?"

Best for: Stalled deals or procrastinators.

Win rate: 38%

Technique #3

The Mutual Close

"We're both aligned on value and timeline. The only question is terms. Can we get procurement and finance on a call to finalize?"

Best for: Deals stuck in legal/finance limbo.

Win rate: 36%
