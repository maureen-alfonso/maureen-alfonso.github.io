SECTION 03

Handling Difficult Clients

Real Estate Sales Executive

When I deal with difficult clients, I remind myself they are not really trying to be difficult. My goal is not to win the conversation, but to help them get clear while still guiding the process with confidence and direction.

The Mindset Behind Every Interaction

Uncertain

Most clients asking hard questions are simply unsure — not uninterested. Clarity is the answer.

Protective

They are protecting their money and their future. That deserves respect, not resistance.

Overwhelmed

Big decisions feel heavy. My role is to simplify the path, not add to the pressure.

How I Respond in Practice

Scenario 01

Client Asking for a Discount

Wants control and reassurance of value
I understand you want the best value. Based on the property and current market, the price is already competitive.
Between getting a lower price and securing the right property, what is your priority right now?
If this already fits your needs, I can help you secure it before someone else does.
Scenario 02

Overthinker Client

Fear of making the wrong decision
I appreciate your questions — it shows you want to decide properly.
From what we discussed, this already matches most of your priorities.
What's holding you back right now? If we can clear that, do you feel ready to move forward?
Scenario 03

"Just Looking" Client

Wants no pressure
No pressure — I'm here to guide you.
If you had to choose today, which property stands out the most for you?
If this one feels right, I can help you secure it while it's still available.
Scenario 04

Cold Client — Follow-Up

Low urgency or distracted
Hi [Name], just checking in. The property you liked is still available.
Since you mentioned looking for [goal], this could still be a strong fit for you.
Would you like me to hold the unit while you decide?
Scenario 05

Skeptical Client

Needs trust and clarity
I understand — it's important to be careful with this kind of decision.
My goal is to give you clear and honest information so you can decide confidently.
What do you need to feel fully comfortable moving forward?
Scenario 06

"I'll Think About It" Client

Avoiding commitment
I understand — it's good to think it through.
Just to clarify, are you still deciding on the property itself or the timing?
If it's more about timing, I can help you secure it now and guide you step by step.
Scenario 07

Confused Client

Internal conflict — logic vs emotion
It sounds like you like the property but still have some hesitation.
Between what makes sense practically and what feels right, where are you leaning more?
If it already feels aligned, I can help you move forward at your own pace.
